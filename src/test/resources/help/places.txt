Usage: brave-search places [-hV] [--no-color] [--quiet] [--verbose] [COMMAND]
Search local places: points of interest, cities, and addresses.
  -h, --help       Show this help message and exit.
      --no-color   Disable ANSI color unconditionally; NO_COLOR disables it too.
      --quiet      Suppress optional diagnostics, never errors.
  -V, --version    Print version information to stdout and exit.
      --verbose    Print diagnostics and resolved non-secret metadata to stderr.
Commands:
  search    Search places with an optional query, an anchor, or neither of the
              two.
  details   Fetch photos, web mentions, and profiles of places by their opaque
              ids.
  describe  Fetch the AI-generated descriptions of places by their opaque ids.
